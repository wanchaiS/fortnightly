The fortnight board in its states

The home screen you chose, plus the states it has to handle. The dock always shows the one thing to do now: clock in or out first, then a missed shift, then the next shift.

Adding shifts and looking at a day

Sheets over the board, with native pickers and Cancel / Add. The timetable look carries into the preview.

Shift details

Tap any shift. What it offers depends on its state; each action is one of the use cases.

Jobs, course breaks and the first run

Settings-shaped content uses native grouped lists. Onboarding explains the overlapping fortnight once, with the timetable itself.

Widget and notification (the extensions)

Outside the app. Lock Screen widgets are drawn by iOS in one colour, so state there is carried by words and the gauge, not by colour. Notification buttons are drawn by iOS; the custom view above them is ours.

Dark Mode and large text

Both are required on iOS. Dark Mode keeps the navy shell and lifts the employer colours. At the largest text sizes, the seven columns can't hold, so the timetable becomes a list per day.
