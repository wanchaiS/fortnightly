Primary use case: clock in from the shift prompt
1At the rostered start iOS delivers the SHIFT_START prompt that RosterShift scheduled when the shift was entered.
2The notification extension reads the shift and fortnight from the App Group store and draws the donut and day row.
3The student taps "Started 5:00 pm" (the rostered time, not the time of the tap).
4ShiftPromptResponder calls ClockIntoShift, which checks its rules; a broken rule shows its message in the prompt instead.
5ShiftRepository saves the shift as on shift through Core Data into Fortnightly.sqlite in the App Group container.
6The use case cancels the remaining clock-in prompts and asks WidgetCenter to reload the widget.
7The widget reads the store again and shows "On shift" with a live timer, on the Home and Lock Screen.
8The prompt shows "Clocked in at 5:00 pm" and closes; the open app hears the remote change and its board shows the shift on.
